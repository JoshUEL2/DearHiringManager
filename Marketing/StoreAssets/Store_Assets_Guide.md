# Google Play Store Asset Specifications & Layout Guide

### 1. App Icon (Required)
- **Dimensions:** 512 x 512 px
- **Format:** 32-bit PNG (no transparency on Google Play; transparent squircle allowed on iOS)
- **Max File Size:** 1 MB
- **Source:** [`Marketing/Logos/DearHiringManager_MasterIcon.svg`](file:///c:/Users/Weari/Documents/Dear%20Hiring%20Manager%20PC/Marketing/Logos/DearHiringManager_MasterIcon.svg)

### 2. Feature Graphic (Required for Google Play Store Listing)
- **Dimensions:** 1024 x 500 px
- **Format:** JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- **Safe Zone:** Center 800 x 400 px (prevent logo or focal elements from being cut off on different screen aspect ratios)
- **Elements:**
  - Game Title Logo on left or center.
  - Key character / resume stamp illustration on right.
  - High-contrast background with subtle brand colors (`#141824` dark slate navy with `#FF3366` accent).

### 3. Store Screenshots (Minimum 4, Recommended 6-8)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1920x1080) for landscape or 9:16 (1080x1920) for portrait.
- **Visual Design:**
  - Slide 1: **"SURVIVE THE JOB HUNT"** (Resume builder / Stat allocation).
  - Slide 2: **"ABSURD INTERVIEW CHOICES"** (Dialogue choice moment).
  - Slide 3: **"CHAOTIC WORKPLACE MINIGAMES"** (Action gameplay).
  - Slide 4: **"CLIMB OR GET FIRED"** (Progression tree / Promotions).
