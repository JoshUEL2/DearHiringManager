TIER 1 • ENTRY

The Slums

Toxic smog, micro-apartments, and predatory debt collectors. Survive by scrubbing dishes and recycling copper wire.

  • Rent: $75/week
  • Jobs: Dishwasher, Scrap Sorter
  • Education: Self-Taught Scripts
TIER 2 • MID-LEVEL

The Suburbs

Suburban sprawl, artificial lawns, and middle management anxiety. The corporate machine demands daily proof of loyalty.

  • Rent: $250/week
  • Jobs: Helpdesk Tech, QA Analyst
  • Education: Tech Bootcamp Certs
TIER 3 • THE SUMMIT

Financial District

Glass towers, private security, and billion-dollar stock options. Only the ruthlessly ambitious hold the corner office.

  • Rent: $1,200/week
  • Jobs: VP of Growth, Fortune 500 CEO
  • Education: Elite MBA & Political Bribes
