# Dear Hiring Manager - Launch Trailer & Video Ad Script

**Target Duration:** 30 Seconds  
**Format:** 16:9 (Landscape for Google Play & Steam) + 9:16 (Vertical cut for TikTok, Reels, Shorts & Rewarded Ads)  
**Music Track:** Upbeat, slightly frantic lo-fi jazz or satirical elevator muzak that abruptly shifts into high-energy chaotic synth beats.

---

### Sequence Breakdown

#### **Scene 1: The Dilemma (00:00 - 00:05)**
* **Visual:** Close-up on the in-game CV / application screen. Rejection counter ticking up rapidly: `Applications Sent: 432 | Rejections: 431`.
* **On-Screen Bold Kinetic Text:** *"Entry level job: Requires 10 years experience."*
* **SFX:** Loud dramatic buzzer / paper shredder sound.

#### **Scene 2: Take Control (00:05 - 00:12)**
* **Visual:** Fast zoom into player choices. The player tweaking absurd stats (Luck, Sanity, Charm) and choosing questionable interview responses.
* **On-Screen Text:** *"Lie on your resume? Or bribe the hiring manager?"*
* **SFX:** Cash register `*cha-ching*`, dice roll, button pop.

#### **Scene 3: Chaos Escalation / Minigames (00:12 - 00:22)**
* **Visual:** Rapid-fire 1-second cuts across different mini-games and event outcomes:
  1. Awkward elevator pitch encounter.
  2. The ID Card printing scene.
  3. The "Sudden Background Check" crisis popup.
* **Audio:** Fast tempo drum hits syncing every single camera cut.
* **On-Screen Text:** *"Every Choice Has Consequences."*

#### **Scene 4: The Climax (00:22 - 00:26)**
* **Visual:** Dramatic slow-motion stamp slamming down onto the cover letter: **"HIRED?"**
* **SFX:** Deep bass impact boom / triumphant corporate fanfare.

#### **Scene 5: End Card & Call to Action (00:26 - 00:30)**
* **Visual:** High-resolution Game Logo (*Dear Hiring Manager*), app icon reveal, and store badges:
  * Google Play Store badge
  * Apple App Store badge
* **Subtitle/Tagline:** *"Can YOU beat the corporate ladder?"*
* **Call to Action:** *"DOWNLOAD FOR FREE NOW"*
